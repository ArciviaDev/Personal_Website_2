The Purpose of the Website Creation

The creation of this website is to record the progression of a learning student over the course of the semester. It is not only for tracking my progress, but to also build up experience to improve my programming and developing skills for future careers. Maybe even for hobbies.

My Motivation for the Website

My motivation for creating this website is simply, creating the website. If I had to add more, it is because I feel creating a website, especially for your own, can be seen as a form of art depending on how much you customize and add more to it. And I myself, am a fan of being able to customize things and make it interactive to users. I had once thought to myself that I should try making a portfolio website so here I am with the beginning journey of how to make one by building up experience.
